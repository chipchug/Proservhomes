Know Before You Book

Common Questions About Fence Installation in Ocean & Monmouth County, NJ

Fence installation in Ocean and Monmouth County is priced by linear foot. Vinyl privacy fence typically runs $35–$55 per linear foot installed; wood privacy fence runs $25–$45; aluminum and chain link are priced by gauge and height. Final cost depends on material, fence style, terrain, and any gate requirements.

Most municipalities in Ocean and Monmouth County require a fence permit, particularly for fences over 6 feet or along property lines. Requirements vary by township — Jackson, Toms River, Brick, and Howell each have their own setback and height rules. We're familiar with local requirements in our service area and can advise on what your project will need before we start.

We install vinyl privacy and picket fencing, cedar and pressure-treated wood fencing, aluminum ornamental fencing, and chain link. Each material has distinct advantages for NJ's climate — vinyl doesn't rot or warp in ocean humidity, cedar weathers naturally, and aluminum is a maintenance-free option for property boundaries.

A typical residential fence installation in New Jersey takes 1–3 days depending on linear footage, terrain, and gate count. Projects requiring permit approval add lead time before install day. We handle the full scope from layout marking to post setting to panel installation.

We recommend having your property surveyed or obtaining a plot plan before installation near a boundary line. Many NJ townships require setbacks from property lines (typically 6 inches to 2 feet depending on the municipality). We work from the client's provided documentation and can coordinate with surveyors if needed.

Yes — sloped terrain is common throughout Ocean County, and we install fences on grades using either a stepped or racked (continuous slope-following) method depending on fence style and your preference. We assess grade during the estimate to recommend the right approach for your property.

All ProServ fence installations come with a workmanship warranty covering post setting and installation quality. Manufacturer warranties on vinyl, aluminum, and treated wood materials are passed through to the homeowner. We'll provide warranty documentation for all materials used on your project.
